# Demo video script (about 2½ minutes)

Record the phone screen (or a browser at phone width) with your voice over it.

**0:00 – 0:20 · The problem**
"Since October 2024, every Indian aged 70 or above gets up to ₹5 lakh of free hospital treatment a year. About 6 crore seniors are entitled, but only 1.36 crore have made the card. Most families simply don't know."

**0:20 – 1:05 · The 2-minute check (in Hindi)**
Open the site. Tap "अभी जाँच करें". Answer: no card → yes, someone is 70+ → not under CGHS → village → SC/ST → no → priority ration card. Show the stamp landing, the reasons, the numbered next steps and the document chips. Point out that it never asked for a name or Aadhaar.

**1:05 – 1:20 · Share**
Tap "WhatsApp पर भेजें" and show the pre-filled message: "a grandchild can check for a grandparent".

**1:20 – 1:55 · Ask Saathi**
Tap the mic and ask by voice: "मेरी माँ 72 साल की हैं, क्या उनका इलाज मुफ़्त होगा?" Show the answer, then tap "सुनें" so it reads aloud. Ask in English: "What if the hospital asks for money?"
Explain: "Saathi is an agent with two tools. It can only state facts from our source-cited knowledge file, and it runs the same eligibility rules as the checker. Aadhaar and phone numbers are removed before any AI call."

**1:55 – 2:15 · The bill you don't pay, and your rights**
Scroll through the receipt section, the 4 steps to get the card, and the 14555 helpline.

**2:15 – 2:35 · Impact**
Open /impact and show the real counters and your pilot numbers. Close with: "Open source, Hindi-first, and built to reach the 4.6 crore seniors who are covered but don't know it yet."
