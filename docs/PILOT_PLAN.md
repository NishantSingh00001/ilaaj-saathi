# Pilot plan: real impact data by 15 November 2026

"Proven impact" is 25% of the score and it breaks ties. Judges want real usage, not intentions. This plan gets Ilaaj Saathi in front of real families in about six weeks and records the results honestly.

## Week 1 (by 4 Oct): go live
- [ ] Open the project in AgentFoundry, push to a public GitHub repo, deploy (Vercel).
- [ ] Create a free Upstash Redis database and add the two keys, so `/impact` starts counting from day one.
- [ ] Add `ANTHROPIC_API_KEY` if you want AI answers (the site works without it).
- [ ] Test the full flow on 3 real phones, including a cheap Android phone, in Hindi and English.

## Weeks 2–6: put it in people's hands
Pick two or three of these. Depth beats breadth: 30 cards actually made is stronger evidence than 3,000 page views.

| Channel | What to do |
|---|---|
| **Your own circle** | Ask relatives, neighbours, your housing society, domestic workers, drivers and shopkeepers: "Is anyone at home 70 or older?" Help them through the check and the Ayushman app. |
| **A card camp** | With a college NSS unit, a residents' association, a senior citizens' club or a religious or community centre, run a 2-hour Vay Vandana card camp. Bring a laptop and phones, and use the Ayushman app or portal with each person entering their own OTP. |
| **CSC operator** | Visit a nearby Common Service Centre and show them the checker. Leave a printed QR flyer at the counter. |
| **ASHA / anganwadi workers** | Through a local health centre, show ASHAs the WhatsApp share. They can send it to families on their list. |
| **NGO partner** | Through your Code for India volunteering, ask for an introduction to an NGO working with the elderly or informal workers. |
| **WhatsApp groups** | Share the link with a one-line message in Hindi: "70 साल से ऊपर के हर व्यक्ति का ₹5 लाख तक का इलाज मुफ़्त है — 2 मिनट में जाँचें". |

## What to record
1. **Automatic:** the `/impact` page (checks finished, seniors found, shares, questions, "I got my card" taps). Take a screenshot every week.
2. **Pilot log** (a simple spreadsheet, one row per session): date, place or partner, people helped, people aged 70+ found, cards made, and how you confirmed it (for example, you saw the downloaded card on their phone). **Never write down Aadhaar numbers, phone numbers or OTPs.**
3. **Before and after:** at each session ask one question before helping: "Did you know everyone over 70 gets free hospital treatment?" Count the yes and no answers. That gives you a real awareness number.
4. **Stories:** one or two short quotes, only with the person's permission (first name and city is enough).

## Ground rules
- People type their own OTP. Never handle anyone's OTP or keep their documents.
- Say clearly that Ilaaj Saathi is an independent helper, not a government service.
- Report only what happened. Small, real numbers score better than large, vague ones.

## Suggested targets (adjust to your time)
- 300+ checks finished on the site
- 50+ families with a member aged 70+ identified
- 20+ Ayushman / Vay Vandana cards made with your help, confirmed
